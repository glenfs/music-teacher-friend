About Clefira.

Clefira builds practical music education software for students, teachers, and studios that want more useful practice between lessons.

Mission

Clefira exists to make musicianship practice clearer, more repeatable, and easier to track. The app focuses on core skills that students need every week: hearing intervals, recognizing chords, reading notes, keeping rhythm, and building confidence through structured practice.

Product philosophy

  • Practice should be specific. Sessions should target a real skill, not just keep a student busy.
  • Feedback should be immediate. Students learn faster when correct and incorrect answers are shown clearly.
  • Teachers need continuity. A good tool should help teachers connect lesson goals to home practice.
  • Privacy should be plain. Local-first storage, no ads, and no third-party tracking are the default direction.
