Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-08 · Clefira v1.0.0

Short version: Clefira stores your data locally on your device by default. If you opt-in to cloud sync, your teacher/student data is encrypted in transit and stored under your account on our cloud provider. We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use third-party analytics or trackers.
Student Edition (Android / Google Play): the Student Edition is fully self-contained on your device. It has no sign-in, no cloud sync, and requests no internet access — nothing you do in the app is transmitted off the device. Sections about cloud sync and Teacher-Edition student data below apply only to the Teacher Edition. The microphone (used only for optional pitch-detection exercises) is processed on-device and never recorded or sent anywhere.

1. Information We Collect

Clefira collects only the information you provide while using the app:

  • Profile information — your display name and skill level (optional; can be skipped).
  • Practice activity — sessions, scores, weak items, time spent. Stored to track your own progress.
  • Teacher Edition only — student data — student names, ages, lesson notes, assignments, repertoire. Entered by the teacher; minor's data is the teacher's responsibility (see COPPA note below).

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • By default — local only. Data lives on your device under the user data folder (Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Clefira\).
  • Cloud sync — opt-in. If you sign in to cloud sync, data is transmitted over TLS to our cloud provider (Supabase) and stored under your authenticated account. You can sign out and delete cloud-stored data at any time from the Cloud Sync dialog.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.
  • We do not include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect telemetry or analytics events without explicit opt-in.
  • We do not access your microphone or MIDI device unless you explicitly enable them for gameplay.

4. Crash Logs

If a fatal error occurs, Clefira may write a local crash log to user://crash_logs/ containing engine version, OS information, and the error trace. These logs stay on your device unless you choose to email one to support.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K)

Clefira's Teacher Edition is designed for use by music teachers in a studio setting. When a teacher records information about a student who is under 13 (US — COPPA) or under 16 (EU — GDPR-K), the teacher is responsible for obtaining parental consent before entering that data. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child's data has been entered without consent, contact us at contact@clefira.app and we will assist with deletion.

6. Microphone and MIDI Access

  • Microphone — used only when you enable Mic Mode or Sight Singing. Audio is processed in real-time on your device for pitch detection and is never recorded or transmitted.
  • MIDI — desktop editions only; used only when you enable Use MIDI Keyboard in the Sight Notes settings. MIDI input is processed in real-time and never transmitted. (The Android Student Edition does not use MIDI.)

7. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Export your data by copying the user data folder.
  • Delete your data by deleting the user data folder.
  • Sign out of cloud sync and request deletion of cloud-stored data via the Cloud Sync dialog or by contacting support.
  • Request a copy or deletion of any data held about you under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted in-app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@clefira.app.
