Payment Processor Comparison

Choosing between Paddle, Polar, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, and friends for paid MusicEd / Clefira Teacher Edition.
Planning doc · 2026-05-21 · Companion to monetization_plan.html · For deciding the payment layer that sits between Supabase auth and Stripe-style billing.

The two big choices

Merchant of Record (MoR) — service handles global tax (VAT, GST, sales tax), chargebacks, refunds, multi-currency. Higher fees (4–6%) in exchange for not thinking about tax compliance.

DIY processor (Stripe direct) — lowest fees (~3%), but you're the merchant of record. You're responsible for collecting and remitting tax everywhere you sell.

For a solo indie shipping internationally, MoR is almost always the right call. The tax handling alone justifies the higher fee — collecting EU VAT, registering for IOSS, and filing returns in 50 countries is a part-time job by itself.

Merchant-of-Record options (handle tax for you)

Service Fees Best for Notes
Paddle ~5% + $0.50 Indie SaaS, software products, subscription apps Most mature MoR. Strong dashboard, recurring billing built in, well-documented API. What most indie SaaS picks today.
Polar.sh 4% + $0.40 Developer tools, GitHub-native projects, modern indie Newer (2023). Open-source, very clean API, indie-dev-friendly. Lower fees than Paddle. Smaller community.
Lemon Squeezy ~5% + $0.50 Was the indie favorite pre-2024 Acquired by Stripe in 2024. Still works, but integration with Stripe's broader product is evolving. Less differentiated now — if going Stripe ecosystem, just use Stripe + Stripe Tax directly.
FastSpring ~5.9% Established software vendors, mid-size indies Mature, popular with bigger indies. Higher fees but very capable. Less indie-friendly UI than Paddle.
Gumroad 10% Creators, one-time digital products, simple lifetime licenses Easiest to set up — practically zero config. But subscription/SaaS support is weaker, and 10% adds up. Good for "Lifetime $299" tier as a side option.
Whop 3% + tx fee Creator subscriptions, communities, course-style products Newer, popular with content creators. Less SaaS-focused — built more for "join my community" tier sales.
All MoRs handle: global tax (VAT, GST, sales tax), chargebacks, refunds, multi-currency, fraud screening. That tax handling alone is worth the higher fee for an indie shipping internationally — collecting EU VAT compliantly is a real job on its own.

DIY (you handle tax yourself)

Service Fees Notes
Stripe 2.9% + $0.30 Lowest fees, most flexible API. You become the merchant of record — you collect VAT/GST/sales tax. Add Stripe Tax (+0.5%) for automated tax calculation; still doesn't shield you legally the way an MoR does, but covers most compliance.
PayPal ~3.5% + $0.49 Widely supported, clunky checkout, recurring subscriptions are workable but not great UX. Many SaaS skip it.
Important reality check: "DIY tax" means registering for VAT in EU member states above the IOSS threshold, sales-tax permits in US states with economic nexus, GST in India / Australia, etc. For an indie selling $9.99/month internationally, this overhead easily exceeds the 2% fee delta vs. an MoR.

Region-specific options

Service Where Notes
Razorpay India Best in India — UPI, NetBanking, local cards. Lower fees than Stripe in India. Worth considering if a meaningful share of your teachers are Indian.
Mollie Europe Strong in EU, multiple local payment methods. Lower friction for European buyers.
Square US / AU / UK / JP More retail / point-of-sale focused. Less natural fit for a SaaS app.

My recommendation

★ Top Pick

Paddle

Most mature MoR. ~5% fees but you stop thinking about tax forever — they collect VAT/GST/sales tax for you and remit it to every government you sell into. Plays well with Supabase via webhooks (the architecture in monetization_plan.html is the same shape: Paddle webhook → Supabase Edge Function → updates subscriptions table).

Best for you because: solo indie, music teachers are international (US, EU, India, AU all major markets), $9.99/mo at low volume = tax compliance is way more expensive in time than the ~2% fee delta vs Stripe.

Strong Alt

Polar.sh

Newer entrant (2023), modern API, cheaper fees (~4%). Worth a serious look if you like minimalist developer-first tools. Open-source ethos. Smaller community than Paddle — fewer Stack Overflow answers when things go wrong, but the product is sharper.

Pick this if: you prefer a cleaner API/dashboard over Paddle's broader ecosystem, and the slightly lower fees matter.

Backup Option

Stripe + Stripe Tax

Lowest total fees (~3.4%). Becomes interesting once you have real volume and tax compliance becomes a known overhead you can scope. Skip this initially — the time you save with an MoR while still small is more valuable than the fee delta.

Skip

Lemon Squeezy (in 2026)

Post-Stripe acquisition, it's no longer the differentiated option it was. If you're going to use Stripe's ecosystem anyway, just use Stripe + Stripe Tax directly. Lemon Squeezy still works, but there's no longer a strong reason to pick it over either Paddle (real alternative MoR) or Stripe (lowest fees).

Decision flowchart

  1. Will you sell internationally from day one? → MoR (Paddle or Polar). Skip Stripe direct.
  2. US/Canada only, OK with sales-tax filings yourself? → Stripe direct + Stripe Tax.
  3. Just want simple one-time / lifetime sales without subscription headache? → Gumroad.

For "MusicEd Teacher Pro $9.99/month sold globally" → Paddle or Polar.
